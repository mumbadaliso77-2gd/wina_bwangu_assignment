About Booth Operations

Agent Mission

To deliver fast, secure, and accurate mobile money and agency banking services to customers across Lusaka District.

Network Tariffs

Standard official fee tiers apply for cash deposits and cash-out operations across Airtel Money, MTN Money, Zamtel Kwacha, Zanaco, and FNB networks.

Agent Standards

  • Verify customer phone details before submission
  • Confirm physical cash count prior to processing
  • Monitor daily e-float and physical cash balance
  • Maintain clear logs for shift reconciliation
  • Protect customer transaction privacy and PIN security

System Overview

Designed for online booth management, enabling agents to process customer transactions in real-time, calculate fee structures, and manage float balances efficiently.
