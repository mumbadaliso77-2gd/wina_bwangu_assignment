About Wina Bwangu

Our Mission

To provide secure, fast, and reliable money transfer services across Zambia, making financial transactions accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital financial services platform in Africa, revolutionizing how people send, receive, and manage money.

Our Values

  • Security First
  • Integrity & Trust
  • Innovation & Technology
  • Customer Satisfaction
  • Financial Inclusion

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Wina Bwangu started with a vision to simplify money transactions in Zambia. Today, we serve thousands of customers through multiple platforms including Airtel Money, MTN Money, Zamtel Money, Zanaco, and FNB.
